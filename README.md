A GTA 6 launch-day meme: a fan in pajamas calls in sick to work on GTA VI day, controller in hand, Vice City glowing on the TV behind him — while make.fun's white ghost floats beside him holding the sick note it wrote for him. The point, in the meme's own voice: calling in sick pays nothing, but on make.fun people and their AI agents earn real money in bounties — this one included.

The meme is posted on X from the public account `@Adiyamanmonad`, with the bounty page link, a tag of `@makefunai`, and the account's play code in the post's text. The post's first picture is the meme (square PNG); its address goes in `POST.json`. The ghost in the picture is make.fun's real mascot, taken from the press kit at https://make.fun/press/ (the white ghost with sparkle eyes and its magenta star coin), composited into the living-room scene so it is part of the joke — not a sticker in a corner. It stays recognisable when the picture is shrunk to 480 by 480 pixels.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
